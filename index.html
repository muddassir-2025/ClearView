ClearView — Privacy Policy

Privacy-First Parental Controls & Family Productivity
Last updated: September 16, 2026  ·  Package: com.muddassir.clearview  ·  Developers: Mohammed Muddassir Ali & Osman Bin Nasir

1. Overview & Architecture

ClearView is an integrated parental-control and family-productivity application designed to provide a safe, disciplined, and enriching mobile environment. Its primary capabilities include:

  • Real-time Content Blocking: Filtering adult content, inappropriate keywords, user-blocked domains, and incognito browsing inside Chrome, the Google app, and in-app embedded WebViews.
  • Curated Media Hub: Safe viewing of public YouTube channels and Instagram creators, Shorts, Reels, and videos, plus offline audio listening without distractions.
  • Islamic Content Hub: Daily Quran verses (with Dr. Mustafa Khattab's English translation and IndoPak Arabic script), Dhikr counter with haptic feedback, Umm al-Qura Hijri calendar, and live Makkah/Madinah broadcasts.
  • Todo & Task Management: Task planning with Normal, Attempted, and Duration-based task varieties, exact alarm reminders, and persistent task history.
  • Phone Limit & Screen Time: On-device daily screen-time monitoring and countdown timers with automated device lock on expiry.
Core Privacy Guarantee: ClearView does not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information. Everything the app inspects to keep you safe runs locally in memory on your device. We require no accounts, no logins, and no cloud synchronization of your personal browsing activities.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe in privacy by design. ClearView is architected so that sensitive personal data is never requested or logged:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, address, or demographic information.
  • No Browsing History: The web pages, URLs, and search queries processed for content filtering are evaluated transiently in memory and are never recorded, stored on disk, or uploaded to any server.
  • No Location Tracking: The app does not request or access GPS, coarse location, or Wi-Fi location permissions.
  • No Contact Lists, Photos, or Private Documents: ClearView never accesses your contacts or photos. Importing audio or media from your device is performed exclusively through Android's system file picker (DocumentsUI) without granting full storage permissions.
  • No Plaintext Passwords: The password that protects your Block settings is hashed locally using SHA-256 with a cryptographic salt; your actual password is never stored or transmitted anywhere.

3. Accessibility Service (Real-Time Content Filtering)

ClearView requires Android's AccessibilityService API (UrlBlockerService) to deliver its core parental-control and web-filtering functionality.

How the Accessibility Service is used:

  • When enabled by the user, the service inspects visible text, URLs, and window properties in real time within supported web browsers (Chrome), search applications (Google app), and third-party apps hosting embedded WebViews.
  • It checks this content against built-in adult safety rules, user-specified blocked keywords, user-defined blocked domains, and blocked YouTube channels.
  • It detects Incognito browsing sessions in Chrome so that filtering cannot be bypassed using private tabs.
  • When a blocked item is detected, the service immediately navigates away from the restricted content, returns to the home screen, or displays the ClearView block overlay.
Strict Ephemeral Processing: All accessibility inspections take place entirely in volatile memory on your device. Screen text and accessibility node structures are never written to permanent storage, never logged, and never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

Note on image screening: Earlier experimental releases referenced an on-device machine-learning image model; current versions rely strictly on real-time keyword, URL, domain, and channel matching.

You can grant or revoke Accessibility access at any time through Android Settings → Accessibility → ClearView.

4. Permissions & System Capabilities

ClearView declares only the permissions necessary to implement its features:

Accessibility Service (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE)
Required to detect and block adult content, keywords, blocked domains, and incognito sessions on screen in real time (see Section 3).
Usage Access / Screen Time (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS)
Prominent Disclosure: Used solely for the Phone Limit feature to read today's screen-on time via Android's UsageStatsManager. Screen time is aggregated locally to calculate remaining daily allowance. ClearView never transmits, shares, or logs your app usage statistics or application history. This permission is optional and must be explicitly enabled in system settings.
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)
Used exclusively to deliver user-requested local notifications: channel upload alerts for saved YouTube creators, Quran verse notifications, and Todo task reminders.
Exact Alarms & Full-Screen Alerts (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED)
Allows Todo task reminders and the Phone Limit expiry alarm to fire at the exact minute set by the user, display full-screen alerts over the lock screen, and re-arm scheduled alarms after the device reboots.
Foreground Services (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, mediaPlayback, specialUse)
Used for: (1) background offline audio playback with lock-screen media controls (mediaPlayback), (2) the persistent Phone Limit countdown notification (specialUse), and (3) sounding the alarm ringtone service when a scheduled Todo alert triggers.
Device Administrator (BIND_DEVICE_ADMIN)
Optional; used only to provide uninstall friction (preventing unauthorized deactivation of parental protection) and to allow the Phone Limit feature to lock the screen (lockNow) when the daily time limit is reached.
Vibrate (VIBRATE)
Provides subtle haptic feedback for each tap on the Dhikr counter and vibrates during Todo alarm notifications.
Internet (INTERNET)
Required to fetch Quran translation texts, retrieve public YouTube and Instagram creator feeds, stream video/audio, and synchronize community blocking rules.

5. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All application state, user preferences, and media files are stored strictly inside ClearView's private sandboxed app directory on your device:

  • Protection Rules: Your blocked keywords, custom website blocklists, and Strict Mode toggles.
  • Security Credentials: Your Block-tab password, stored exclusively as a cryptographic SHA-256 hash.
  • Todo & Task History: Your tasks, task categories, completion timestamps, and permanent edit history.
  • Media Subscriptions & Cache: Followed YouTube channels and Instagram creators, video metadata caches, and watch/progress state.
  • Offline Audio: Downloaded audio tracks and cached album thumbnails for local offline listening.
  • Quran & Worship Data: Bookmarked verses, reading positions, downloaded translation texts, and Dhikr counter tallies.
  • Phone Limit Settings: Daily target durations, timer state, and usage preferences.
  • Cached Rule Updates: Locally cached channel blocklists and rule snapshots received from the backend.

Uninstalling the app or clearing app storage deletes all of this data immediately and permanently.

6. Third-Party Services & Network Activity

ClearView makes network requests only to provide user-requested features and to keep protection rules updated:

  • ClearView Community Backend: ClearView queries our dedicated backend service (websites-checking-confirmed-pipes.trycloudflare.com) to download updated community blocking rules (keywords and blocked channel lists), verify whether a YouTube channel is blocked, and submit user-reported channel blocks. No personal user profiles, device identifiers, or browsing histories are associated with these requests.
  • YouTube / Google LLC: Used to retrieve public video metadata, public channel RSS feeds, video thumbnails, and play content via YouTube's official embedded player. On-device stream resolution for offline audio utilizes open-source extractor technology. All YouTube interactions are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Instagram / Meta Platforms: Public creator profiles, Reels, and photo posts are retrieved via public RSS feeds, public RSS-Bridge mirrors (such as rss-bridge.org), and public Instagram embeds. ClearView does not require, accept, or store Instagram user credentials or logins.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC): To help diagnose technical issues and monitor app stability, ClearView includes Google Analytics for Firebase. This service collects standard pseudonymous device and app telemetry (such as app open frequency, session durations, device model, operating system version, and approximate location based on IP address) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. No personal browsing content, blocked keywords, or user passwords are ever sent to analytics.
  • jsDelivr CDN & Open Quran API: The English translation ("The Clear Quran" by Dr. Mustafa Khattab) and IndoPak Arabic script are downloaded once from the open-source fawazahmed0/quran-api repository hosted on the jsDelivr CDN (cdn.jsdelivr.net) and cached locally for offline reading.

7. Children's Privacy

ClearView is a family-oriented parental-control and productivity tool intended to be set up and administered by parents and guardians.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your territory). Because all content filtering and task tracking occur locally on the device without user accounts, there is no transmission or central storage of data concerning children who use the device.

8. Security & Data Protection

Your data is protected by architectural principles:

  • All persistent data is confined within Android's private, sandboxed application storage, inaccessible to other apps on non-rooted devices.
  • All network communications with backend APIs, CDNs, and media sources use industry-standard HTTPS encryption (TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3).
  • Password protection for the security dashboard uses SHA-256 cryptographic hashing.
  • Screen analysis performed by the Accessibility Service operates strictly in volatile memory and is never written to disk or network sockets.

9. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Because user data is stored strictly on your device, you retain absolute ownership and control over your data:

  • Clear All Data: Open Android Settings → Apps → ClearView → Storage → Clear Data / Clear Storage. This resets the application and permanently deletes all stored preferences, keywords, todos, and downloaded audio.
  • Uninstall: Removing ClearView from your device permanently erases all associated local files.
